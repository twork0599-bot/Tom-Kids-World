Tommy and the Little Lost Duck

One sunny morning, Tommy was walking through the park when he heard a tiny sound near the pond. “Quack, quack!” A little yellow duckling was standing beside a patch of flowers. It looked around, but its family was nowhere to be seen.

Tommy stayed calm and spoke softly. “Hello, little duck. Are you looking for your family?” The duckling gave a small quack. Tommy knew he should not take the duckling away, so he stayed nearby and asked a park helper for help.

The park helper smiled kindly and brought a small box with a soft towel. Together, they watched from a safe distance. Soon, a mother duck appeared from behind the reeds with three ducklings following her. The little duckling hurried toward them.

“Quack, quack!” called the mother duck. The family was together again. Tommy waved goodbye and felt happy that he had asked a grown-up for help.

On his way home, Tommy remembered an important lesson: when an animal needs help, be gentle, keep a safe distance, and ask a trusted grown-up. And that made his sunny day even brighter.
